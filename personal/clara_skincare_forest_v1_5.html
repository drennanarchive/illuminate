❧ Getting StartedGentle six-week introduction guide⌄

The weekly schedule above is your eventual rhythm, not a reason to start every active at once. Patch-test each new product separately, and slow down if needed.

Week 1 · Matrixyl

Patch-test, then use every other morning if comfortable.

Week 2 · Settle in

Matrixyl daily if tolerated.

Week 3 · Add retinal

Start with two nonconsecutive evenings, on dry skin.

Week 4 · Stay gentle

Maintain twice-weekly retinal and focus on comfort.

Week 5 · Add vitamin C

Patch-test first, then use two mornings, preferably on different days while assessing tolerance.

Week 6 · Assess

Vitamin C 2–3 mornings and retinal 2–3 nights only if skin is comfortable. You can take longer than six weeks.

✧ Notes & Skin WisdomMask frequency, irritation, photos, and reminders⌄

🌸 Biodance: Once a week to start, or every other week if you prefer. First test with a shorter wear time; follow packaging directions. Skip retinal that evening.

☀ SPF: Every morning; reapply when appropriate during sun exposure.

🌿 Sensitivity: Stop the newest product if redness, bumps, or burning appear. Your prior Hyalu B5 reaction doesn't establish B5 as the culprit.
